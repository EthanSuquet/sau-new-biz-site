This presentation outlines a two-year program to give St. Ambrose University and Mount Mercy University distinct, credible positions in a crowded market.

We begin with insight driven research, turn what we learn into a platform and Brand campaign for each university, then carry each story into the academic programs that drive enrollment.

For St. Ambrose University, that story is built around Faith, Passion, professional breadth and the idea of becoming someone who can make an impact in the world. For Mount Mercy University, it is ambition in service of others, and answering the next great need. All rooted in the Mercy tradition.

From there, we work program by program to reverse St. Ambrose University’s enrollment decline, turn Mount Mercy University’s stability into growth and strengthen retention at both universities.

This plan closes with a clear view of what to grow, launch, redesign or retire, measured against inquiries, applications, deposits and students who stay.

the story

The Insight: Students don’t want to choose between a successful future and a life that matters. Each institution offers a distinct path to both.

The Human Truth: I want a future I can be proud of, and I need to believe this college can help me get there.

The Opportunity: Is not to make St. Ambrose University and Mount Mercy University sound bigger or more alike. It is to make each one unmistakable and magnetic.

St. Ambrose University should own the story of becoming, showing how rigorous learning, close faculty relationships, exploration, and responsibility help students grow into capable, purposeful people setting out to better the world.

Mount Mercy University should own ambition in service of others, turning the Sisters of Mercy legacy into a living story of students building professional expertise and using it to improve lives.

Both brands should share a commitment to preparation, purpose, belonging, and student success, but enter that territory through different emotional doors. These promises must then be proved through programs, student experiences, outcomes, cost clarity, and support. The goal is not simply more applications. It is clearer choice and a stronger fit. Find the right students, not just any students.

Our Recommendation: Build two unique brands around one shared foundation and two distinct stories: St. Ambrose University is about who you can become and your impact on the world. Mount Mercy University is about what your ambition can make possible for others.

the process

Base Process

  • Baseline: initial discovery, build internal brand team, establish clear objectives and expected outcomes
  • Immersion: conduct market research to understand the market, dive deep into the products (i.e. academic programs, campus experience), understand the audiences and what motivates them and to influence their choices, pressure test the Human Truth
  • Synthesis: pull out the DNA from the research and begin the process of creating two magnetic brands
  • Positioning: create a distinct brand positioning for each university, allowing each to claim a unique spot in the market where the brand can thrive, grow, and stay relevant as the market shifts and changes
  • Integration: creative process begins, starting with brand visualization, story creation, and development of an integrated communication plan
  • Execution: creative campaigns are produced and launched, the media plan is executed across the market
  • Evaluation: analytics review, refinements to the messaging and placements
